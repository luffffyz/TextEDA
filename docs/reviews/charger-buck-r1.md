# Review of charger-buck, round 1

Target `board:charger-buck`. Roles: power, architect, compliance, layout, structural. The round covers both descriptions of the board, the schematic and `design/pcb/charger-buck.teda`. Notes are open: no decision yet. The cross-board note is filed here because the target enum has no `system:` ([role-reviews.md](../guides/role-reviews.md)).

- power (2026-10-23): the board declares `current VBAT 3A` and `current VOUT 3A` (`design/sch/charger-buck.teda:35-36`) behind a 4 A adapter (`design/modules/charger-power.teda:3`). Both maxima cannot hold at the low end of `voltage SYS 9V..19.8V` (`:32`): 3 A into a 12.6 V pack is ~42 W at the input, and 5 V/3 A out is ~17 W, so ~59 W total needs about 14.7 V at 4 A (assuming 90 % efficiency, which is an assumption to confirm, not a measured number). Between 9 V and roughly 14.5 V the input-current regulation has to give up charge current — so either DPM is intended, in which case 3 A is not the worst case and the constraint states the wrong thing, or the adapter range must be narrowed. Settled by: one sentence naming the corner the design was checked at.
- power (2026-10-23): `current VOUT 3A` equals TPS5430's own `rated_current current 3A` (`assets/components/regulators/tps5430ddar.teda`), so the rail has no margin at the declared maximum. Either state the derating policy the project follows (80 % would make it 2.4 A) or lower the constraint to what the load needs. Settled by: a derating rule, or a lower number with a reason.
- power (2026-10-23): `M2.U1.5` (ENA) appears in no net row (`design/modules/buck-5v.teda:20-26`) and is the only non-MCU pin left unconnected in the project (`texteda check --json`). If "enabled whenever input is present" is the intent, the design should say so — a tie, or a written acceptance of a floating input. As written, no file records the intent and the reviewer cannot tell it from a missing connection. Settled by: a decision, either way.
- architect (2026-10-23): the board exposes the port `DC_IN` on net `ADAPTER_P` (`charger-buck.teda:17`, `:27`), but `ADAPTER_P` has no voltage constraint: the input range exists only in a comment ("19V adapter", `charger-power.teda:3`), while the capacitors around it are rated 35–50 V. No system uses this port today, so nothing else constrains it either. A declared external interface whose limits are stated nowhere is worse than no port. Settled by: a `voltage ADAPTER_P` constraint, or removing the port until something uses it.
- architect (2026-10-23): `power-chain.teda` joins charger `VOUT_5V` (4.85–5.15 V) to mcu-board `DC_IN` (4.5–5.5 V) — the intervals overlap, so the voltage side is consistent — but neither side states the current expected on that link; mcu-board constrains only `V3V3`. The related note is in `mcu-board-r1`. Settled by: one current constraint on either side of the link.
- compliance (2026-10-23): `[compliance]` lists `CE` and `RoHS` (`project.toml`) while the board carries a 3-cell Li-ion charger at up to 3 A. Decide whether pack-level standards apply (IEC 62133, UN 38.3 for transport) or record that the pack is outside this project's scope. Settled by: the pack supplier's certificate, or a scope statement.

- layout (2026-10-23): the input capacitor of the buck is 7 mm from the pin it buffers. `M2.C1` sits at (23, 18) and `M2.U1` at (16, 18) (`design/pcb/charger-buck.teda`, `place`), so the loop that carries the pulsed current — C1 → VIN (U1.7) → PH → D1 → GND — encloses roughly 10 × 5 mm. That loop, not the switching frequency, sets the switch-node ringing and most of the radiated emission of a 3 A converter. Settled by: moving C1 against the VIN/GND pads, or stating that emissions are not a requirement for this board.
- layout (2026-10-23): the feedback divider and the geometry disagree. `connect` declares `M2.R1.1` joined to `M2.C3.1`, i.e. the divider taps the output at the capacitor; but the only VOUT copper in `trace` starts at R1 (14.5, 25) and runs about 28 mm down and around to the capacitors at x ≥ 36 (C3 at 36, 20; C4 at 43, 14). Either there is copper the `trace` section does not show, or the regulator senses through a 28 mm trace. Nothing checks that a declared `connect` pair is covered by drawn copper, so this cannot be caught mechanically today. Settled by: drawing the VOUT run the `connect` section implies, or moving the divider tap to the output capacitor.
- layout (2026-10-23): the PCB description covers 9 of the board's 61 parts. The module `buck-5v` has 9 instances and `charger-power` has 52 (`design/modules/*.teda`), and only the 9 appear in `place`. Nothing in the file says the layout is partial, no diagnostic reports unplaced instances, and the comment that explains it ("The buck module's parts on the board") is prose. An agent reading `place` and `connect` will conclude the board is laid out. Settled by: a marker for a partial description, or placing the rest.
- structural (2026-10-23): the board declares no `physical_only` section: no mounting holes, no fiducials, no test points. A 50 × 36 × 1.6 mm board that has to be assembled and brought up normally has all three, and nothing marks this one as a trial. Note that the IR cannot hold a mounting hole today either: a `physical_only` entity has no position yet (`docs/backlog.md` F38), so this is a spec hole, not only an omission in this file. Settled by: the mounting and test-point requirements, or a statement that this is a layout exercise.
- structural (2026-10-23): there is no `keepout`: no height limit and no enclosure envelope. The tallest part is `M2.L1` (IHLP4040) and its height appears nowhere, so the board cannot be checked against a case later. Settled by: a height limit, from the enclosure or from an explicit decision that there is none.
- structural (2026-10-23): the schematic declares five external ports (`AC_RET`, `BAT`, `DC_IN`, `GND`, `VOUT_5V`) but no terminal is placed and no pad stands for one: the PCB has no port or connector geometry. Where power enters and leaves the board is undecidable from the design, which is also why the `DC_IN` note in this round cannot be answered geometrically. Settled by: connector parts in the library, or test pads declared in `physical_only`.
- structural (2026-10-23): verified, so that it does not have to be checked again — the stackup sums to the declared thickness: 0.035 + 1.53 + 0.035 = 1.60 mm (`stackup`, `board_thickness`).
