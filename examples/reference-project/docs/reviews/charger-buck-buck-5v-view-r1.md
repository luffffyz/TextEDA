# Review of the charger-buck print page `buck-5v`, round 1

Target `page:charger-buck/buck-5v`, artifact the `view` description (`views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda`). Roles: readability, hardware, structural. Verdict: `accepted` — see `charger-buck-buck-5v-view-r1.decision.toml` (2026-10-25, owner `luffffyz`). The notes stand as observations: the two field collisions and the three crossings are accepted as defects of this drawing, the undrawn power path as a stated gap, and the field-versus-wire rule has since landed as `view.text-on-wire` (see the notes' `→ addressed` lines). The page is the board's only fully wired drawing: it places all 9 instances of module M2 and draws all 6 of that module's nets, and `texteda view-metrics examples/reference-project charger-buck/buck-5v` reports 15 wires / 726.4 mm / 3 crossings / label min gap 1.3 mm / no wire through a body. → addressed 2026-10-25: after the redraw below the same command reports 15 wires / 761.0 mm / 0 crossings / label min gap 6.2 mm / text min gap 6.2 mm / 0 wires through a body. The schematic of the same board is `charger-buck-sch-r1`, its layout `charger-buck-pcb-r1`; this round looks only at what a reader of the drawing sees. The method is [role-reviews.md](../../../../docs/guides/role-reviews.md); the record format is [ADR-0033](../../../../docs/adr/0033-review-records-and-attribution.md).

- readability (2026-10-25): the Reference field `M2.U1` is bisected by the SYS run. The field sits at the symbol's own `field Reference at=0,10.16` (`assets/symbols/project/dc-dc-soic8.teda:10`), i.e. x = 140 above the body on this page, and `SYS M2.C1.1 M2.U1.7 120.0 90.0 140.0 90.0` (`views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda:32`) drops into VIN at the same x: in the render the wire passes between the `M2` and the `U1` glyphs. No check sees it — the readability pass compares text with text (`view.text-overlap`, `view.label-collision`), a wire with a symbol body (`view.wire-crosses-symbol`) and a wire with a wire (`view.wire-crossing`), never a field with a wire (`packages/core/src/view/readability.ts`). Settled by: moving the Reference field in the symbol, or entering VIN from the left so no run lands on the field.
  → addressed 2026-10-25 (the blind spot, not the drawing): the checker now sees this class — `view.text-on-wire` reports it on this page as 1.3 mm of SYS through the Reference field. The field is still where the symbol puts it, so the note stays open until someone moves it.
  → addressed 2026-10-25 (the drawing): the field moved in the symbol — `field Reference at=12.7,10.16` in `assets/symbols/project/dc-dc-soic8.teda` puts `M2.U1` above the body's right corner, clear of the VIN drop at x = 140. `view.text-on-wire` no longer reports it.

- readability (2026-10-25): the Value field `TPS5430DDAR` is crossed twice by its own pins. The field sits at `field Value at=0,-10.16` (`assets/symbols/project/dc-dc-soic8.teda:11`), i.e. y = 115.15 below the body, and the two ground connections leave the body at x = 137.5 (pin 6) and x = 142.5 (PAD, pin 9) down to y = 120 (`views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda:25`), so both stubs run through the letters. Settled by: the same one-line field move, or dropping the pads either side of the text.
  → addressed 2026-10-25 (the blind spot, not the drawing): `view.text-on-wire` reports both ground runs through the Value text (1.3 mm and 2.5 mm). The drawing is unchanged.
  → addressed 2026-10-25 (the drawing): `field Value at=12.7,-10.16` moves `TPS5430DDAR` to the right of the two ground drops (x = 137.5 / 142.5), so neither stub runs through the letters.

- readability (2026-10-25): `M2.BOOT` at (157, 100) and `M2.PH` at (165, 100) (`views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda:43`, `:44`) are the page's tightest pair at 1.3 mm, and at sheet scale the two read as one string, `M2.BOOT M2.PH` — on exactly the pair of nets where confusing them means a bootstrap capacitor across the switch node instead of to it. Settled by: moving one of the two labels; `view-metrics` should then report a larger minimum gap.
  → addressed 2026-10-25: `M2.BOOT` moved to (157, 104) and `M2.PH` to (162, 111.5) on its new run; `view-metrics` reports label min gap 6.2 mm (was 1.3 mm).

- readability (2026-10-25): the ground return and the VOUT run are parallel for 55 mm, 2 mm apart: `GND M2.C4.2 M2.R2.2 … 238.0 120.0 238.0 173.8` (`views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda:23`) against `VOUT M2.C4.1 M2.R1.1 240.0 91.2 240.0 146.2` (`:34`), and the `VOUT_5V` port at (240, 146.2) is reached by a horizontal that crosses the ground return at (238, 146.2). A reader who follows VOUT to the port passes through GND, which is the same picture as a shorted output. Settled by: returning the divider ground on the far side of R1/R2, or moving the port off the ground run.
  → addressed 2026-10-25: the divider is inverted (R2 above R1), so the ground leaves R2.2 at its top pin, rises on x = 238 and joins the bus — the crossing at (238, 146.2) is gone. `VOUT` now arrives from a column at x = 246 into R1's bottom pin with the `VOUT_5V` port on that column: the parallel run is 26 mm at 8 mm apart, and no reader follows VOUT through GND to reach the port.

- readability (2026-10-25): three wire crossings are drawn with no junction dot (`view.wire-crossing` on `views/charger-buck/buck-5v.view.teda:21`, `:23`, `:26`): GND × M2.VFB at (120.0, 107.9) — the feedback divider reads as grounded; GND × VOUT at (238.0, 146.2) — as above; M2.BOOT × M2.PH at (157.0, 101.2) — reads as a bootstrap short to the switch node. The renderer's rule is right (the absence of a dot is how the drawing says "not connected"), so the fix cannot be drawn: only routing can settle it. Settled by: rerouting the VFB and BOOT runs and re-running `texteda check` until the three warnings are gone.
  → addressed 2026-10-25: `texteda check examples/reference-project` now reports 0 `view.wire-crossing` on this page. BOOT × PH went away when `M2.PH` was rerouted under the body to the diode cathode; GND × VOUT when the divider was inverted (note above); GND × VFB when C1 moved to (126, 88), off the VSENSE pin's left exit, so the feedback run can leave to x = 110 without crossing the cap's ground drop. Wires 761.0 mm, 0 crossings, 0 through a body.

- structural (2026-10-25): the two field defects above are not authoring mistakes this page can avoid. Fields are placed by the symbol (`field Reference` / `field Value` in the `.teda` symbol), `place` rows accept only `unit` as an extra column (`spec/schematic-view.md` §4), and no readability rule compares a field with a wire — so when a symbol puts its fields on the pin columns, neither the page author nor the checker can see the collision. Settled by: a decision to allow a per-instance field offset on `place` (a view-language change), or to add a `view.text-on-wire` check.
  → addressed 2026-10-25 (half): the check exists — `view.text-on-wire`, with the symbol's Reference / Value boxes now in the geometry the checker reads (`packages/renderer` `schematicGeometry.fields`, `spec/schematic-view.md` §9.2). There is still no per-instance field offset: the only repair today is editing the symbol.

- hardware (2026-10-25): no drawing shows the board. The page draws the 6 nets of module M2 and nothing else; 30 of the board's 36 nets are drawn on no page (`view.nets-not-drawn`), because `views/charger-buck/charger-power.view.teda` is a placement study — 52 instances, 4 ports, 0 wires, 36 nets undrawn (`texteda view-metrics examples/reference-project charger-buck/charger-power`). A reviewer of the charger can see the buck and cannot see the power path that feeds it; the design is drawable today, it is simply not drawn. Settled by: wiring `charger-power` one net at a time in its own round, after which `view.nets-not-drawn` either shrinks or is replaced by a statement of what the page leaves out.
